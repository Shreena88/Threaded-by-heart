Meet the Heart Behind the Thread

"Threaded by Heart" is the soulful creation of my beloved mother — a passionate artisan who pours love and tradition into every handmade crochet piece. What began as a humble hobby in our cozy home soon blossomed into a heartfelt journey of creativity and joy.

With years of skill, patience, and an eye for detail, she crafts each item as a unique expression of warmth. From baby cradle cords to gift items, her hands tell stories through thread.

Her mission? To bring smiles and a touch of handmade magic into people's lives — one stitch at a time.
